## INVITATION TO PARTICIPATE

You are invited to participate in a research study. Participation is entirely voluntary. Please read this form carefully before deciding whether to participate.

---

## PURPOSE OF THE STUDY

We are creating a dataset of eye movements and facial gestures to train and evaluate EyeNav — an eye navigation system that allows people to control computers and devices using only eye movements. This research may benefit people who cannot use traditional input devices (keyboards, mice, touchscreens) due to motor disabilities.

**What we are building:** A dataset of eye movement recordings labeled with the navigation intent that prompted them (e.g., "scrolling," "selecting," "reading"). No such dataset currently exists. This dataset will be used exclusively to train and evaluate the EyeNav system.

---

## WHAT YOU WILL BE ASKED TO DO

If you agree to participate:

1. **You will sit in front of a camera** — either your own webcam or a provided camera — for approximately 45 minutes.
2. **The application will display text prompts** describing eye movements to perform (e.g., "Look left," "Blink twice slowly," "Look at the top-left corner").
3. **You will perform the prompted movements** to the best of your ability. There are no "wrong" answers — everyone's eye movements are different.
4. **The application automatically records** your facial video during each prompt.
5. **You may stop at any time** without penalty or consequence.

The session will take approximately **45–60 minutes**.

---

## WHAT DATA IS COLLECTED

**During the session, the application records:**

- Video of your face (640×480 pixels, 30fps) — limited to the camera field of view
- Audio: **NOT recorded**
- Timestamps of when each prompt was shown and completed
- Your device's operating system type (no other device information)
- Session metadata: lighting condition, camera model (self-reported)

**What is NOT collected:**
- Your name, email address, or any contact information (unless you choose to provide it for compensation purposes)
- Screenshots of your screen content
- Any application data on your computer
- Location data

---

## HOW YOUR DATA WILL BE USED

Your recordings will be:

1. **Used to train and evaluate AI models** — the video will be processed to extract eye position, gaze direction, and movement patterns.
2. **Stored securely** — encrypted at rest using AES-256, on servers located in [Country/Region].
3. **Retained for 10 years** — consistent with research data retention standards.
4. **Potentially published** — anonymized statistical summaries and aggregate results may be published in research papers. **Raw video will NOT be published.**
5. **Shared with research partners** — under strict data sharing agreements, anonymized extracted features (not raw video) may be shared with academic research partners.

---

## PRIVACY AND ANONYMIZATION

- Your face video will be processed to extract numerical features (gaze coordinates, landmark positions). The extracted features are not linked to any identifying information.
- Raw face video will be accessible only to authorized research staff.
- Any publication or model release will not include raw video.
- You will be assigned a random participant ID (e.g., P04721). Your identity will not be linked to this ID in any shared dataset.

**Biometric data notice:** This study collects video that captures your face. Under GDPR and equivalent laws, facial recordings are classified as **biometric data** and receive the highest level of protection. You may request deletion at any time.

## VOLUNTARY PARTICIPATION AND YOUR RIGHTS

- **Participation is completely voluntary.** You may refuse to participate or withdraw at any time without consequence.
- **Withdrawal:** You may stop at any time during the session. If you withdraw before the session ends, your data will be deleted within 30 days upon request.
- **Data deletion:** After the study concludes, you may request deletion of your data by contacting the team at the email below. Deletion requests are honored within 30 days.
- **Access:** You may request a copy of the data collected about you.
